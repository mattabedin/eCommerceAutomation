Pricing FAQ

Common questions.

Are there really no transaction fees? +
Forge takes 0% on every plan, forever. You'll still pay your payment processor (typically Stripe at 2.9% + 30¢). We don't add anything on top.
What counts as an "AI generation"? +
A generation is one AI action — drafting a product description, generating a brand, replying to a ticket, suggesting a price. Most stores use 500–2,000/mo. Pro's 10,000 cap covers nearly everyone we've onboarded.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes. Monthly plans cancel anytime; annual plans are pro-rated. You keep ownership of your store, products, customers, and data — export anything as CSV or via the API.
Do you offer a free trial of Pro? +
Yes — 14 days, no credit card required. You get the full agent stack, custom domain trial, and unlimited generations during the trial.
What if I outgrow Pro? +
Scale is for brands at $50K+/mo who want multi-store, custom agents, headless API, and SOC 2 docs. Email sales@forge.com — most upgrades are done in 24 hours.
Is my data safe? Are you SOC 2? +
Yes. SOC 2 Type II report available on Scale. All plans use PCI Level 1 payment processing, encrypted at rest and in transit. We never train on your data.
Can I migrate from Shopify? +
Yes — our import wizard pulls your products, customers, orders, and theme references in one click. Most migrations finish in under 30 minutes.
